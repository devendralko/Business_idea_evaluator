Paste a business idea. A four-role panel — Market · Pricing · Positioning · Revenue — scores it and calls GO / REWORK / KILL, right here on screen.

Where do I get a key? & is it safe?

Create a key at openrouter.ai/keys. It's saved only in your browser's local storage on this device and sent directly to OpenRouter — never to anyone else. Set the model to any OpenRouter model ID, e.g. openai/gpt-4o-mini, anthropic/claude-3.5-haiku, or google/gemini-flash-1.5. The fields are per-browser, so a fresh copy has no key.

Ctrl/⌘ + Enter to run.
